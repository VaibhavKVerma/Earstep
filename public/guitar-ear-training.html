Why guitar players get stuck

You can memorize chord shapes and still not hear what you are playing. Guitar ear training is the missing piece. You do not need every scale on day one. You need C versus D until they are obvious, then the next note.

What Earstep does

  • Plays acoustic guitar, electric guitar, piano, or a sine wave
  • Asks you to name the note
  • Lets you tap the fretboard to find what you heard
  • Walks public-domain phrases like Twinkle and Sa Re Ga Ma

This is music learning built for beginners, not a full theory course. Read what ear training is or how to learn notes.
