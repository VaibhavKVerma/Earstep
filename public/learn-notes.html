Start with two notes

C and D are enough. When you can tell them apart without guessing, add E. That is how music learning sticks — a small set, lots of listening.

Names you can use

  • Western: C D E F G A B
  • Solfège: Do Re Mi
  • Sargam: Sa Re Ga Ma

The letter and the pitch go together later (C4 is middle C). First learn the name, then how high it is.
