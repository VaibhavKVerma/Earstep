About Earstep

By Vaibhav Verma · Updated 14 September 2026

Earstep is a free website for learning notes by ear. It is made by Vaibhav Verma. The trainer is a practice tool. The guides are the written teaching.

Why this site exists

Most ear trainers I tried threw every chromatic note at me on the first session. I could tap answers and still not hear C versus D. The idea behind Earstep is smaller: two notes, then a third, then height, then the guitar neck. If you want to figure out songs later, you need that order more than you need a leaderboard.

I built the site for beginners who hold a guitar or sit at a piano and want the sound first. There is no account. Your streak and note history stay in this browser. That is not a slogan — it is how the app is stored. Clear the site data and the record is gone.

What is original here

The Journey map, the two-note progression, the Low / Middle / High drill, and the fretboard that plays the tapped pitch are written for this project. The lessons are short on purpose. The longer “why” lives in the guides so a quiz screen is not pretending to be an article.

Songs used for practice are public-domain phrases (for example Twinkle and simple sargam lines), not commercial recordings. Instrument sounds are short practice samples, not a streaming library.

Advertising

If ads appear, they appear on the written guides — not on the listening or answering screens. The trainer is a tool. Google’s publisher rules ask that ads sit next to publisher content. That is why the app itself does not load AdSense.
