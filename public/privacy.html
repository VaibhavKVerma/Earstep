Privacy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Earstep is a free ear-training website at earstep.netlify.app. It is made by Vaibhav Verma.

Progress on this device

Practice history, settings, and streaks stay in your browser (localStorage). There is no Earstep account. Clearing site data, changing browsers, or using another device removes that local progress.

Advertising

Earstep uses Google AdSense to show ads on browsing screens (for example Home, Lessons, Stats, and after a session). Ads are kept off screens where you are listening or answering.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses data for ads and ad settings to opt out of personalized ads.

What we do not do

Earstep does not create user accounts, does not ask for a password, and does not sell your practice data. We do not run a separate analytics product today.
