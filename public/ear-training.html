Ear training for beginners

Ear training means you hear a sound and know what it is. Earstep is a free way to learn notes by ear, one pair at a time.

Start ear training

Most music learning apps dump every note on you at once. That is why people quit. Here you start with C and D. When those feel easy, you add more.

What you practice

  • Note recognition — hear a pitch, name the letter
  • Low, middle, and high — the same letter at different heights
  • Guitar — find the note you heard on a fretboard
  • Intervals and short public-domain songs
  • Western names, solfège, or sargam

How to begin

Open Earstep, listen, and name the note. Replay as much as you want. Progress stays on this device. There is no account.
